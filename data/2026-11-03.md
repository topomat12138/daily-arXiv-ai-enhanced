### [16] [Pair density waves via interband pairing --- a perspective from superfluid weight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41687)
*Yi-Jian Hu, Yi-Fan Jiang, Wen Huang*

**Category**: cond-mat.supr-con  
**Abstract**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41687)

**TL;DR**: PDW order can be triggered purely by interband Cooper pairing even when quantum-geometric contributions to superconducting stiffness vanish. By computing the mean-field superfluid weight (SW) in two canonical models — an s-wave Fulde–Ferrell model with Zeeman splitting and a spinless honeycomb lattice with nearest-neighbor f-wave pairing — the authors show SW is strongly suppressed and can become negative (signaling instability toward spatially modulated pairing). **Tags**: `pair density waves (PDW), interband pairing, quantum geometry, unconventional superconductivity, Bogoliubov Fermi surfaces`

<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>
Pair density waves (PDW) are superconducting states that exhibit a spatially modulated pairing order parameter, corresponding to Cooper pairs carrying finite center-of-mass momentum. While some recent studies have emphasized the role of quantum geometry in stabilizing PDW order in certain multiband systems, we here present a systematic investigation of an alternative, geometry-independent mechanism: PDW order driven purely by interband Cooper pairing. Using the superfluid weight (SW) as a diagnostic, we study two canonical models. The first is the classic Fulde-Ferrell model of an $s$-wave superconductor subject to a Zeeman splitting field, which realizes pure interband pairing between two rigidly shifted bands; the second is a spinless honeycomb-lattice model with nearest-neighbor $f$-wave pairing, a modified version of which was recently found to exhibit PDW instability in density-matrix renormalization-group simulations. In both models the SW receives no quantum geometric contribution at the mean-field level, yet it can be greatly suppressed or even turn negative, signaling a tendency toward pairing with spatial phase modulations. For the first model, we establish that the SW is increasingly suppressed as the Zeeman field grows, drastically turning negative upon the emergence of extensive gapless excitations, i.e., the Bogoliubov Fermi surfaces. For the honeycomb $f$-wave model, we demonstrate, both analytically and numerically, vanishing quantum-geometric SW and an invariably negative total SW. Our results thus establish the SW as a qualitative indicator of the tendency toward PDW order, and provide a microscopic rationale for the interband-pairing-driven PDW instability.
