<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>
We study the radial electric field around a singly quantized vortex in a charged BCS superconductor, when the Higgs amplitude mode is driven by a uniform terahertz (THz) electromagnetic pulse. In a local-density approximation the pairing gap vanishes on the vortex axis at all times, so the particle density on the axis cannot oscillate, whereas the curvature of the density profile around the axis does. Starting from the electrostatic-lens formalism of our previous work, we show that this converts the electrically neutral Higgs oscillation into an alternating radial electric field that vanishes on the axis. When the electrostatic screening length is much shorter than the vortex core, we obtain the field in closed form: it has a ring-shaped profile, with a maximum at a distance of the order of the coherence length from the axis, and the Higgs drive modulates it by a fraction $2η$, where $η$ is the relative oscillation of the gap. We also discuss how the thermodynamic factor entering the field changes toward the BCS-BEC crossover.
