### [1] [Chern Numbers from Symmetry-Enforced Zeros and Dispersion Shifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25028)
*Yoonseok Hwang, Henry Davenport, Frank Schindler*

**Category**: cond-mat.mes-hall  
**Abstract**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25028)

**TL;DR**: The paper identifies and reads out Chern topology by analyzing zeros and local vorticities of wave-function overlaps between a Chern band and chosen reference states; crystalline rotation symmetry constrains those zeros/vorticities and weak local perturbations produce energy shifts ~|overlap|^2 that let one extract the zeros (and bound the quantum metric) without measuring Berry phases.  
**Method**: Construct wave-function overlaps between a Chern band and freely chosen reference states (including compact localized states); use crystalline rotation symmetry and angular-momentum constraints to fix overlap zeros and their vorticities modulo rotation order; probe the overlap via weak local perturbations that induce band-dispersion shifts proportional to |overlap|^2, enabling readout of zeros and bounds on the quantum metric. Applicable to multicomponent/multiband systems and reference states that vanish at some momenta.  
**Tags**: `quantum geometry, topological band theory, symmetry-protected topology, flat-band physics`

<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>
Wave-function zeros and their local vorticities provide a complementary characterization of Chern topology to more commonly used Berry-curvature formulations. Using wave-function overlaps between a Chern band and freely chosen reference states, we show that crystalline symmetry can enforce their zeros and fix their vorticities modulo the rotation order from the angular momenta of the band and reference state. We further show that weak local perturbations produce band-dispersion shifts proportional to the absolute square of the overlap, allowing its zeros and local structure to be read off directly from the dispersion. Combined with crystalline symmetry, this can identify Chern topology without measuring wave-function phases. Our construction applies to multicomponent and multiband wave functions and allows reference states whose momentum-space representation may vanish at some momenta. This permits compact localized states to be used directly as reference states and makes use of their known real- and momentum-space properties in flat-band systems. The dispersion shift near an overlap zero further provides a lower bound on the quantum metric at the zero.

### [2] [Topological (in)equivalence encoded in zeros of wavefunction overlaps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25037)
*Carolina Paiva, Bruno Mera, Roni Ilan, Raquel Queiroz*

**Category**: cond-mat.mes-hall  
**Abstract**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25037)

**TL;DR**: The paper shows that the relative topological class between two gapped band structures is encoded in the determinant of their occupied-subspace overlap matrix: its zeros (where the map is noninvertible) define a line-bundle section whose singular connection assigns integer charges by phase winding. These charges reproduce differences of Chern numbers in class A and the Z2 relative invariant in class AII (recovering the Kane–Mele Pfaffian), with crystalline symmetries constraining zero locations; illustrated on Haldane, Kane–Mele and Qi–Wu–Zhang models.  
**Method**: Construct the determinant of the overlap matrix between occupied subspaces as a global section of a line bundle over the Brillouin zone, define a singular connection whose curvature is localized at zeros of this determinant, associate integer charges via phase winding of each zero, and relate the total/half-zone charge parity to Chern-number differences (class A) and Z2 relative invariants (class AII); demonstrate on model Hamiltonians and consider crystalline symmetry constraints.  
**Tags**: `topological band theory, Chern insulators, Z2 topological insulators, quantum geometry, crystalline symmetry constraints`

<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>
Topological equivalence between two gapped band structures is determined by whether their occupied states can be continuously deformed into one another without closing the energy gap or breaking the protecting symmetries. We show that this relative topology can be read directly from the overlap matrix between the two occupied subspaces, in a way that treats Chern and $\mathbb{Z}_2$ invariants within a single framework. The determinant of the overlap matrix defines a global section of a line bundle over the Brillouin zone, which vanishes at the momenta where the map between the subspaces fails to be invertible. A singular connection, whose curvature is concentrated at these zeros, assigns each zero an integer charge given by its phase winding. In two-dimensional class A, the total charge equals the difference between the Chern numbers of the two systems. In class AII, time reversal pairs zeros of opposite charge, and the relative $\mathbb{Z}_2$ invariant is the parity of the total charge in half the Brillouin zone; the Kane-Mele Pfaffian is recovered as a special case. The zeros coincide with the gap closings of an interpolation between the two systems, and crystalline symmetries constrain their positions and charges. We illustrate the construction in the Haldane, Kane-Mele, and Qi-Wu-Zhang models, including cases where rotation symmetry alone enforces the zeros.
</details>
