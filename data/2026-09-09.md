<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>

By replacing the standard spatial Laplacian with the Riesz fractional Laplacian $-(-\nabla^2)^{α/2}$ (where $1<α<2$), we prove that the vortex-antivortex interaction transitions from logarithmic confinement to a stronger power-law confinement of the form $V(r)\propto r^{2-α}$.
