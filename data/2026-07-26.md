### [1] [Exceptional-Point Geometry of Weak Topological Boundary States](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21148)

**Category**: cond-mat.mes-hall  
**Abstract**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21148)

**TL;DR**: Weak topology is formulated geometrically by analytically continuing the Bloch Hamiltonian into complex momentum space and identifying exceptional singularities: edge states map to exceptional points (complex k in one direction), corner zero modes to exceptional curves (complexifying both momenta), and compact localized states appear when exceptional roots collapse to the origin. <details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>
In this article, we demonstrate that weak topology can be formulated geometrically in terms of exceptional singularities of an analytically continued Bloch Hamiltonian. A general plaquette chiral model in two dimensions serves as a minimal realization of dual weak topology, possessing two independent families of weak topological invariants, one for each spatial direction. The weak-topological edge states correspond to exceptional points in complex momentum space, while corner zero modes emerge from exceptional curves obtained by complexifying both momenta. Compact localized states arise when the exceptional roots collapse to the origin. This framework provides a unified complex-momentum description of edge, corner, and compact localization.

### [2] [Ideal Bands in Tight-Binding Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20624)

**Abstract**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20624)

**Tags**: `topological bands, Chern insulators, quantum geometry, tight-binding models, Berry curvature`

<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>

We first provide an analytic construction of isolated Chern-ideal bands with Chern number $|\mathrm{Ch}|=1$ in finite-band models with exponentially decaying hopping. This construction applies only when at least two orbitals have different embedded positions (modulo lattice vectors), complementing the previously known construction for $|\mathrm{Ch}|>1$.
