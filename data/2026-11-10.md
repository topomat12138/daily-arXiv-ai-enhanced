### [1] [Real Topology on Symmetry-Invariant Loops Enforces Superconducting Pairing Zeros](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12454)
*Hongyu Lu, Hao Jin, Wang Yao*

**Category**: cond-mat.supr-con  
**Abstract**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12454)

**Tags**: `nodal superconductivity, topological superconductivity, pairing-obstruction mechanisms, symmetry-protected topology, vortex physics`

<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>
Normal-state topology can obstruct superconducting pairing and give rise to gapless nodes in the quasiparticle spectrum. However, existing pairing-obstruction mechanisms largely rely on Fermi-surface topology or global Bloch-band topology. Here, we establish a new and general mechanism: one-dimensional (1D) real topology, defined on antiunitary-symmetry-invariant loops, enforces pairing zeros without requiring Fermi-surface or global Bloch-band topology. We derive sufficient conditions and lower bounds for pairing zeros across diverse pairing channels, for both zero and finite Cooper-pair momenta. The theorems apply to systems of arbitrary spatial dimensions without requiring the pairing bands to be globally isolated. We further show that such topologically enforced pairing zeros, if isolated, necessarily carry odd vorticity and can give rise to Bogoliubov-de Gennes nodes and zero-energy boundary modes, as demonstrated in a minimal two-orbital model. Our results reshape the understanding of how normal-state topology can obstruct the superconducting order, highlight the significant role of 1D real topology beyond single-particle band phenomena, and provide a general framework for identifying candidate materials hosting nodal superconductivity.
