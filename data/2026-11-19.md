  <summary>Abstract</summary>

We focus on odd-parity Josephson trijunctions, where the presence of a trapped fermion induces a phase winding in each trijunction. Connecting these trijunctions constrains their shared phases, resulting in a long-distance phase coherence where the frustrated configurations dominate the low-energy physics. Thus, superconductivity in infinite-layer nickelates can be reliably described within a single-band Hubbard framework, while the accurate description of inter-orbital properties such as (optical) conductivities requires the inclusion of at least the interstitial-$s$ band.
