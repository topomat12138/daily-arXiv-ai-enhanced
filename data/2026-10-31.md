<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>

We compare Wyckoff deformations of atomic configurations with symmetry-preserving adiabatic deformations of occupied states, both with and without adding common occupied atomic orbitals. Explicit examples show that stable Wyckoff equivalence and adiabatic equivalence at fixed filling do not imply each other. In spinless $Fm'm'm'$, an Euler class distinguishes stably Wyckoff-equivalent configurations. These groups preserve spacetime orientation and have a point whose site-symmetry group contains $222$, $\overline1'$, or $\overline4'$.
