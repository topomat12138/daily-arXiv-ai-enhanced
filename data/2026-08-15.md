<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>

Their mixing is doubly forbidden --- by point-group symmetry at zero momentum and because the two reside in the amplitude and phase sectors of the order parameter respectively, by particle--hole symmetry at every momentum. In that regime, the coupling computed here would set the splitting between the hybridized branches. We discuss the experimental implications of these results.
