Two junctions sharing a heated island split the bias hysteretically and, even at zero bias, lock into opposite voltages encoding the sweep direction. With a load and current bias, the automaton accesses states spanning junction polarity combinations, including zero voltage; the magnetic field sets which are available and the bias history selects the occupied one.
